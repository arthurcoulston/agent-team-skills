# Cultivate organizational memory selectively

Keep an experience only when you can name the future decision it could improve.
Retention creates retrieval, context, maintenance, privacy, and staleness costs;
an event does not earn permanence merely because it happened repeatedly or was
expensive.

## Apply a retention test

Before promoting an observation, record:

1. the prospective consumer and decision;
2. the smallest substance that decision needs;
3. the source, observed conditions, limitations, and material contrary cases;
4. the event or date that requires reevaluation.

If these are unknown, leave the episode in its existing record or let it go.
Frequency is evidence of recurrence, not importance: retain a rare safety or
consent exception when its consequence warrants it, and discard a common detail
that changes no future choice.

Reconstruct the causal claim before turning a result into a lesson. One success
does not show which practice caused it, and a good run after a failure does not
establish the repair. Keep unresolved explanations as hypotheses. Use
[successful-practice transfer](../transferring-successful-practices/SKILL.md)
when the proposed lesson should travel beyond its originating case.

## Give retained material one authoritative home

Place an item by what it does now, not by the format it arrived in:

- **Live state:** a current commitment, permission, owner, dependency, queue
  position, or operating fact that must change when reality changes.
- **Durable evidence:** dated observations, sources, outcomes, rejected
  approaches, exceptions, and correction history needed to audit a claim.
- **Role memory:** a worker's heuristic for a recurring decision, carrying its
  applicability and a route to the evidence.
- **Reusable guidance:** instructions justified for a defined class of future
  work, with conditions, exceptions, and a reevaluation trigger.

Keep one authoritative substance and link to it from indexes and dependent
records. A retrieval route is not another copy. Sensitive history and prior
consent remain evidence unless current authority makes them live state; storage
never turns an old permission into standing permission.

Use [context and retrieval design](../designing-context-and-retrieval/SKILL.md)
when changing how a continuing role discovers these layers. Placement and
behavioral change are separate decisions: first make the basis findable, then
test whether changed context or instructions alter downstream action. Use
[context-change evaluation](../evaluating-context-and-skill-changes/SKILL.md)
for consequential adoption decisions.

## Consolidate without erasing

Consolidate when several items answer the same future question. Preserve source
links, consent constraints, material boundary cases, contrary observations, and
the reason for the resulting rule. Mark displaced material as superseded and
redirect its consumers; do not silently rewrite the record. Prefer a short rule
with traceable evidence over an exhaustive narrative loaded into every run.

Retire or demote material when its consumer disappears, its conditions change,
new evidence defeats it, a stronger mechanism replaces it, or measured retrieval
and context cost exceeds demonstrated value. Retirement removes active influence,
not necessarily the evidence. Keep a tombstone or correction link where deletion
would break provenance or leave obsolete copies appearing current. Revalidate the
substance; changing only its review date is not renewal.

After consolidation or retirement, check the decision paths that used the old
item: indexes, standing context, live records, dependent guidance, and active
work. A clean archive is not success if a consumer still acts on the retired
version.

## What this method rests on

Controlled agent studies show both curated experience gains and automatic-memory,
skill, context-cost, and stale-premise failures. They do not test this four-place
taxonomy, consent-aware retention, or a complete organizational lifecycle. Read
the [supporting synthesis](../../knowledge/selective-organizational-memory.md)
before transferring their measurements.

**Draft.** No consumer-effectiveness experiment has been run against this skill,
and the evidence supplies no universal review cadence or retention quantity.
