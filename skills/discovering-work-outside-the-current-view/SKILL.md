# Discover work outside the current view

A team can execute every visible assignment well while pursuing the wrong
intervention or overlooking people its current service does not reach. Run a
bounded outside-view pass separately from ordinary prioritization: first widen
the option set, then decide what to do.

## Trigger and bound the pass

Open a pass when beneficiary evidence changes, outputs continue without outcome
movement, a material assumption fails, a credible external model appears, or a
scheduled strategic review arrives. State the question, evidence budget, owner,
and stopping condition. Continuous exploration is not an outside view.

## Reconstruct the beneficiary's problem

Restate the continuing purpose, intended beneficiary change, current course,
and the assumptions that make that course plausible. Look beyond the current
service and its active users: seek non-users, exit and failure cases,
intermediaries, affected people, and the ways the outcome is achieved without
your organization. Record groups you could not reach and the limits of proxies.
Treat participant accounts as evidence about experience, not authority over the
team's decision.

## Generate genuinely different courses

Vary more than features. Consider a different beneficiary segment,
intervention mechanism, delivery channel, partner or owner, and enabling
another organization instead of doing the work yourself. Include maintaining
the current course, stopping it, and choosing none of the generated options.

## Compare every course on the same basis

For the current course and each alternative, record:

- the expected beneficiary change and causal assumptions;
- supporting and contrary evidence, including who is missing;
- required authority, access, capability, and coordination;
- downside, reversibility, cost, and opportunity cost;
- the observation that would disconfirm the course.

Use outcomes from similar undertakings when a defensible reference class
exists. State why its members are comparable; do not let a convenient class
erase differences central to the mission.

## Probe, decide, and reconnect to operation

Prefer the smallest safe probe that distinguishes the leading hypotheses.
Adopt, revise, park, or reject each course with reasons. Retain rejected options
whose rejection conditions may change. Put selected courses through ordinary
work selection and propagate any adopted direction change through commitments,
roles, context, and interfaces.

[The supporting synthesis](../../knowledge/forming-an-outside-view.md) explains
the evidence, mission transfers, and limits. User research can overrepresent
reachable people, foresight can multiply plausible stories without ranking
them, and reference classes can be badly chosen. Triangulation and reversible
probes reduce those risks; they do not prove that the best course was found.

No reviewed evidence shows that this combined workflow improves a continuing
agent team's strategy, and no consumer-effectiveness test has been performed.
