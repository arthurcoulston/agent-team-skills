# Transfer a successful practice

Treat a local win as a candidate causal claim, not yet as reusable guidance.
Reconstruct what happened before deciding that the practice should travel.

## Reconstruct the originating case

Record the outcome, the prior baseline or credible comparison, the practice
actually used, surrounding conditions, simultaneous changes, costs, and
plausible alternative explanations. If the run cannot identify what caused
the result, keep a hypothesis and gather another comparison instead of
broadening the practice's authority.

Compare at least one materially different case. Name the relation the cases
share and the surface details that differ. Extract the smallest mechanism that
could explain both outcomes; separate its required core from elements a new
recipient may adapt. Similar wording or task labels are not evidence of the
same mechanism.

## Choose the next boundary to test

Promote only across a boundary the evidence has crossed:

- **Task:** keep the practice local when only the originating use is supported.
- **Role:** test another task with the same recurring decision and recipient
  capabilities.
- **Team or organization:** test a different owner or operating context,
  including coordination and authority effects.
- **Shared collection:** require materially different organizations, models,
  or environments relevant to the claim, or state the narrower applicability.

Distribution does not strengthen evidence. High-consequence, irreversible, or
adversarial use needs more diverse evidence than a low-cost reversible pilot.

## Write the transfer claim before testing

State the mechanism, expected observable benefit, cost and failure signal,
changed dimensions, fixed core, allowed adaptations, and matched baseline.
Check that the recipient has the knowledge, tools, authority, incentives, and
feedback loop the mechanism requires. Name defeat conditions: circumstances
under which the practice should not be used or the result should not count.

Prefer a related case not used to formulate the rule. Compare with and without
the practice when feasible, keeping the outcome measure and operating limits
matched. Inspect cost as well as success. If added context may explain an agent
result, include a length-matched irrelevant control. Widen one boundary at a
time so a failure remains interpretable.

## Decide without erasing contrary cases

After the test, choose one result: keep local; repeat because attribution is
unresolved; promote only at the tested altitude; narrow or adapt; or retire.
Preserve negative transfers and material exceptions beside the claim. Reopen
it when the model, harness, tools, recipient, authority, environment, or desired
outcome changes.
