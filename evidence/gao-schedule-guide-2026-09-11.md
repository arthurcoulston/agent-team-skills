# Reading — GAO Schedule Assessment Guide

GAO describes an integrated schedule as a model connecting all activities
needed for an outcome. Dependencies must be logically linked; otherwise a
delay does not propagate, float is unreliable and the critical path can be
wrong. The guide also calls for actual progress and remaining work to update
the schedule, rather than retaining a baseline fiction. Its practices assess
large programme schedules, so they support dependency tracing but do not show
that detailed scheduling is proportionate for small or exploratory agent work.
