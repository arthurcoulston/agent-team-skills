# Reading — NIST contingency planning

The guide identifies May 2010 but not a publication day, so the machine-readable
date remains unknown rather than inventing precision. NIST starts recovery design from mission and business processes: identify
consequence, prioritize system resources, set recovery-time and recovery-point
objectives, choose strategies, then test, exercise and maintain the plan. A
restart is not sufficient if the prioritized function has not been restored
within its consequence-shaped bounds.

The guide governs US federal information systems and carries documentation and
organizational formality that should not be imposed on every agent mission. The
transfer used here is narrower: prioritize the useful function, declare time and
state-loss tolerance, and exercise the recovery path. This is a paraphrased
reading of the published guide, not a continuity exercise.
