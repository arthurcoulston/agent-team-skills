# Reading — requirements-change impact analysis

NASA directs teams to document requirements changes and analyze effects on the
whole software system and project, not only the immediate artifact. The named
impact areas include architecture, design, interfaces, concept of operations,
higher- and lower-level requirements, safety, reliability, performance and
needed expertise. A review body may perform the analysis before disposition.

This is normative engineering guidance, not an evaluation of organizational
strategy change. It supports searching across dependent artifacts and owners;
it does not establish that an agent team needs NASA's approval structure or
that the proposed reconciliation ledger is effective.
