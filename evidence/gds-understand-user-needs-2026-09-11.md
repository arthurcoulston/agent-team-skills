# Reading — understand the whole problem before the solution

The standard asks teams to understand users' full context rather than only
their interaction with government. It recommends secondary evidence,
qualitative and quantitative research, analytics, and inexpensive throwaway
prototypes for testing risky assumptions. It warns that starting from a
solution can conceal the problem.

This supports problem-first inquiry and reversible probes. It does not compare
strategic-discovery methods or establish their effectiveness for agent teams.
