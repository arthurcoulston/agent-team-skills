# Reading — Structure for Reading, Prose for Writing v1

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20786v1). Reading scope: System, experimental setup, conditioning comparison and discussion; v1.

A deployed tender-authoring study reports that a prose-to-XML instruction transformation harmed judged writing quality, despite useful markup in reading stages. This is a narrow paired intervention, not a clean universal test of serialization alone: organization and salience changed too. The study uses withheld commercial documents, an incompletely identified non-frontier model and model-based quality judgments, limiting reproducibility and transfer. It is a counterexample to universal format advice, not grounds to ban XML.

This is a paraphrased reading record, not an independently reproduced result.
