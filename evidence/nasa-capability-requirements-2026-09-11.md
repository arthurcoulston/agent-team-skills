# Reading — NASA requirements and enabling products

NASA derives technical requirements and enabling products from stakeholder
expectations, measures of effectiveness, constraints, external interfaces,
operational scenarios, and allocations between people and systems. It warns
that requirements developed without stakeholder iteration can yield the wrong
solution. This supports value-to-setup traceability, not the effectiveness of a
capability-request template.
