# Reading — GovS 002 Project Delivery

The UK government standard applies across portfolios, programmes and projects,
with governance, roles, planning and control, and solution delivery as linked
areas. Version 2.1 added explicit treatment of transition, use and disposal.
The accompanying framework emphasizes knowing what belongs in each portfolio
and considering cross-functional dependencies. This supports maintaining
ownership and delivery beyond task completion, but the source prescribes a
government control environment rather than testing a lightweight agent ledger.
