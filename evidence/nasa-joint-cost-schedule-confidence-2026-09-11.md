# Reading — joint cost and schedule confidence

NASA describes Joint Cost and Schedule Confidence Level analysis as integrating
cost, schedule, risk and uncertainty to estimate the chance of meeting both a
target cost and target finish. Correlation matters because cost and schedule
exposure can move together. The process makes assumptions, sensitivity and
reserve levels explicit and treats reserve use as governed rather than free.

This supports sizing contingency from a stated exposure model and
reconsidering remaining exposure after a draw. It does not support importing
NASA's confidence levels, modeling burden or governance into a small agent
team. Appendix J identifies February 2015 but not a day, so the machine-readable
`source_date` remains unknown rather than inventing precision.
