# Reading — Agentic AI scientists and problem selection

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08956v1). Bisht et al. Version 1,
2026-05-09. Reading scope: abstract and version metadata only.

The position paper argues that present agentic research systems are oriented
toward bounded execution rather than autonomous scientific discovery. It
identifies problem selection as a missing capability and proposes mechanisms
that may narrow it, including optimizing what is measurable, missing tacit and
failure knowledge in training corpora, and preference optimization that
compresses output diversity toward consensus.

## Limits

This is a position paper and reports no experiment of its own. Its mechanisms
are hypotheses useful for framing a gap, not measured causes or proof that a
particular agent cannot select valuable problems. Only the abstract was read.
