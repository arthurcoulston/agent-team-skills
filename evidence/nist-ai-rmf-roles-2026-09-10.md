# Reading — NIST AI RMF roles and accountability

[Source](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/). NIST AI
100-1, published 2023-01-26. Read 2026-09-10: the Core overview and Govern
categories 1–3.

The framework treats governance as continuous across an AI system's lifecycle.
Govern 1.5 calls for ongoing monitoring and periodic review with defined
responsibility; 1.7 calls for safe decommissioning. Govern 2 requires empowered,
trained accountable actors: 2.1 requires clear roles, responsibilities and
communication lines, while 2.3 leaves executive leadership responsible for risk
decisions. Govern 3.1 asks that lifecycle decisions receive diverse disciplinary
and experiential perspectives; 3.2 distinguishes human-AI roles and oversight.

This is a voluntary consensus framework, not a controlled comparison of
organizational forms. It supports explicit accountability, review and retirement;
it does not show that adding a role improves performance or supply a role-design
algorithm. NIST notes that AI actors responsible for one process part may lack
visibility or control over other parts, a reason to specify interfaces rather
than assume shared context.
