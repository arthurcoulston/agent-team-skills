# Reading — W3C PROV-O

PROV-O models entities, activities, and agents and relates them through use,
generation, derivation, attribution, association, and delegation. This supports
retaining who supplied or transformed an item, what it was derived from, and
which activity produced a later artifact.

The recommendation says provenance can support assessments of quality,
reliability, or trustworthiness. It does not say that a provenance record makes
the described content true or the named agent authorized. The operative
guidance preserves that limit.
