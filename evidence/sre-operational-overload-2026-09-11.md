# Reading — handling operational overload

The chapter describes overload as urgent operational work preempting project
work, which can remove the team's ability to reduce future operational demand.
It recommends measuring the actual workload, prioritizing and dropping work,
protecting interrupt-free engineering time, and treating toil reduction as a
project. Google SRE teams cap operational work at 50% of an engineer's time.

The chapter also warns that perceived overload and actual overload differ and
that measures are not one-size-fits-all. Its evidence is first-party practice
and two retrospective Google team cases, not a controlled comparison. The 50%
cap is therefore a local institutional rule, not a general allocation constant.
