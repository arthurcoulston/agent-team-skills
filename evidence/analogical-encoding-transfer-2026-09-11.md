# Reading — analogical encoding and transfer

Gentner, Loewenstein, and Thompson (2003) tested novice negotiation learning.
Holding two examples constant, instructed comparison produced full schemas in
42% of participants versus 13% after separate study. In a later negotiation,
contingent contracts occurred in 90% of guided-comparison pairs, 70% of open-
comparison pairs, 55% of separate-case pairs, and 37% without training.

The small student samples support structured comparison under these conditions.
They do not test organizational routines, agent guidance, or a universal
minimum number of examples. The copy gave no precise publication day.
