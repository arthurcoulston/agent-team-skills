# Reading — learn needs beyond the current service

The guidance asks teams to learn who likely users are, what they seek to do,
how they do it now, their frustrations, and the outcome they need. It recommends
existing evidence, observation and interviews, and people inside and outside
the organization who know likely users. It explicitly includes atypical users
and people who struggle with existing services. Opinions from stakeholders and
the team remain assumptions rather than substitutes for user evidence.

This supports looking beyond an artifact and its easiest-to-see users. It is
normative government-service guidance, not a comparative trial.

The page reported publication in 2016 and update in 2017; the retained reading
did not verify an exact revision date.
