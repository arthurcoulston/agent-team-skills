# Reading — NASA Technical Authority

[Source](https://www.nasa.gov/technical-authority/). NASA Office of the Chief
Engineer, page dated 2023-07-18. Read in full on 2026-09-10.

NASA separates Programmatic and Technical Authorities as a checks-and-balances
structure. Named individuals receive formally delegated authority to provide an
independent view and approve changes or waivers within their requirements.
Crucially, the program or project manager still remains responsible for safe
conduct and successful outcome. The structure therefore separates a bounded
decision right without splitting ultimate result ownership.

This is an operating governance description from a safety-critical public
agency, not an experiment and not an agent-team design. Its transferable
mechanism is independent challenge plus an explicit decision boundary; its
formal hierarchy and waiver process should not be imported by default.
