# Reading — a taxonomy for far transfer

Barnett and Ceci (2002) separate what is transferred from the context in which
it is tested. Their dimensions include domain, physical and temporal setting,
function, social setting, and response mode. Evidence can be near on one
dimension and far on another.

The review provides neither a pooled effect nor an organizational promotion
procedure, and important combinations remain untested. The copy gave no
precise publication day.
