# Reading — school health implementation

Evans, Scourfield, and Murphy found substantial local variation in a UK school
health programme. The evidence could not cleanly distinguish warranted
adaptation from drift, and most included studies observed two years or less.
This does not establish an adaptation procedure for agent teams.
