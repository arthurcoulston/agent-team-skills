# Reading — CEO-Bench v2

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18543v2). Chen, Narasimhan et al. Version
2, revised 2026-07-22. Reading scope: abstract and version metadata only.

The benchmark gives an agent an indefinite business objective rather than a
task list: operate a fictional subscription company for 500 simulated days,
starting with $1 million and no customers, through a programmable Python
interface in a partially observable, noisy and evolving market. The abstract
reports that only Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 4.8 finish above
the starting balance, and that every evaluated model remains below a
rule-based baseline. It also reports sophisticated forecasting code from the
strongest agents, evidence that execution of individual actions can coexist
with poor strategic choices.

## Limits

Only the abstract was read. The record does not support method details absent
from that abstract, an estimate of performance in real businesses, or a claim
about months of wall-clock autonomous operation. The environment is simulated
and narrow, and the result compares evaluated models with one rule-based
baseline. No result was reproduced.
