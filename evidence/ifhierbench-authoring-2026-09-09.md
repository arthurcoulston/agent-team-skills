# Reading — IFHierBench v1

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27912v1). Reading scope: Benchmark construction, results and limitations; v1.

IFHierBench tests synthetic structured-output tasks with nested constraint scopes. Performance declines with more demanding hierarchy and composition. The hierarchy in this paper concerns where output requirements apply; it is not the authority ordering of system, developer and user instructions. The study motivates checking that conditions and exceptions remain attached to their intended scope. It does not establish that flattening every document or banning nested headings improves real team instructions.

This is a paraphrased reading record, not an independently reproduced result.
