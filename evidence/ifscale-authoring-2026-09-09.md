# Reading — How Many Instructions Can LLMs Follow at Once? v1

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11538v1). Reading scope: Task construction, instruction-density results and limitations; v1.

IFScale increases the number of keyword-inclusion constraints in an English business-report task and measures compliance across multiple models. Compliance deteriorates at high densities. Its constraints are deliberately simple and do not represent all kinds of operating instructions. The result provides no universal safe number of rules, tokens or headings. It motivates measuring competing requirements and their combined effect instead of accumulating instructions without checking consumption.
