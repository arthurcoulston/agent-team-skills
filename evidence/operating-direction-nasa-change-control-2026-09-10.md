# Reading — change control and traceability

NASA's handbook treats a change as a controlled revision to a baseline. Its
typical process evaluates a request, decides and assigns actions, executes the
approved change, verifies the release package, releases final documentation
and closes the request. Changes affecting an external interface require
coordination with affected stakeholders. Configuration status accounting keeps
current and historical documentation, change status and final disposition.

This is engineering configuration management for systems with formal
baselines, not evidence that a continuing organization should impose a change
board or approval gate on strategy. The transferable mechanism is narrower:
trace a direction change through affected interfaces and records, verify its
implementation, and retain the former basis rather than assuming an announced
change has propagated.
