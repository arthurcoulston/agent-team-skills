# Reading — Magenta Book evaluation guidance

The 2026 [Magenta Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html)
separates inputs, activities, outputs and outcomes. It recommends designing
monitoring and baselines before implementation, against a causal account that
states assumptions and context. Contribution analysis can test that account
and alternatives when an experiment is infeasible, but does not prove the
counterfactual.

This supports comparing a report with prior measures, dated decisions and
observed results. The guidance assumes a defined intervention and trained
evaluators; a continuing mission may change its intervention and lack a
credible counterfactual. Data collection can burden participants, alter
behavior and introduce bias, so more records are not automatically better.
