# Reading — applying OECD evaluation criteria thoughtfully

[OECD guidance](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/applying-evaluation-criteria-thoughtfully_543e84ed-en.html)
treats relevance, coherence, effectiveness, efficiency, impact and
sustainability as complementary lenses, not a universal checklist. Results
include intended and unintended outputs, outcomes and impacts. Context may
change, so fidelity to an old plan is not success by itself.

This supports mission-specific qualitative judgment and explicit disagreement
about evidence or values. The criteria are normative guidance, developed
mainly for development cooperation; they do not validate this collection's
report instrument, prescribe weights or resolve strategic uncertainty.
