# Reading — When Do Prompt-Side Agent Playbooks Transfer? v1

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05778v1). Reading scope: Sections 3–7, including controls and stated limits; v1.

The study freezes playbooks and tests transfer across tasks, models and runtime conditions. Results vary substantially; service-task route claims are limited after multiplicity correction. An exploratory XBench experiment with one frozen artifact shows repeated querying and delayed stopping under runtime change. The evidence supports compatibility testing and tracking operational cost. It supplies neither a universal transfer rule nor a validated automatic selector, and the runtime example should not be generalized to all long-context agents.
