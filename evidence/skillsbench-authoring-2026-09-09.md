# Reading — SkillsBench v4

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12670v4). Reading scope: Abstract and evaluation design; v4.

SkillsBench reports benefits from curated skill packages across its terminal-task evaluation and also evaluates self-generated skills. Its findings support testing whether authored expertise changes task performance, rather than assuming that any skill-shaped text helps. The tasks and supplied packages are bounded; natural discovery and indefinite team operation are not established by these results. This reading builds on the paper's abstract and the previously inspected evaluation description.
