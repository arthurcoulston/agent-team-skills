# Reading — data-driven performance reviews

GAO surveyed performance officials at 23 US federal agencies, clarified their
responses, interviewed officials at five selected agencies and reviewed
guidance and agency records. Most agencies reported quarterly or more frequent
reviews, advance analysis of relevant data, assessment of progress and named
follow-up actions. Identifying follow-up actions was positively correlated with
officials' reported performance impact; the design cannot establish that the
review practices caused better outcomes.

The statutory quarterly cadence belongs to US agency priority goals. It is not
a universal interval for a continuing team. The transferable structure is a
review triggered often enough for the decision at stake, with relevant owners,
evidence prepared before the review, at-risk assumptions or goals examined,
and resulting actions documented and tracked.
