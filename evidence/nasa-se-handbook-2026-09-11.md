# Reading — NASA Systems Engineering Handbook

The handbook connects interface management with integration, verification and
validation. Interface working groups coordinate responsible parties and retain
interface decisions, assumptions, anomalies and agreements. Product
verification asks whether the end product conforms to requirements; validation
asks whether it works for intended use; transition delivers the realized product
to the next level or customer. These distinctions transfer to multi-owner
deliveries, while NASA's engineering controls and documentation volume do not.
