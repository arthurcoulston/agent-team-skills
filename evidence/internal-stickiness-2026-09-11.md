# Reading — internal stickiness

Szulanski (1996) models transfer as source-recipient replication through
initiation, implementation, ramp-up, and integration. Difficulty was associated
especially with causal ambiguity, recipient absorptive capacity, and an
arduous source-recipient relationship.

The retrospective perceptual measures establish associations and stages, not
causal estimates. The copy gave no precise publication day.
