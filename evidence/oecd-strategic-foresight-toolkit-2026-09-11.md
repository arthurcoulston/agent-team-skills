# Reading — use multiple futures to challenge assumptions

OECD describes the toolkit as developed through a participatory,
multi-stakeholder process and organized around challenging assumptions,
exploring multiple possible futures, and preparing robust or adaptable policy
rather than predicting one future.

This supports deliberate alternative generation and stress-testing under
change. Its setting is public policy, and the material read does not show that
foresight selects the best intervention or organizational design.
