# How to form an outside view of a continuing mission

Service discovery begins with the beneficiary's outcome and current way of
achieving it, not the organization's proposed solution. UK Government guidance
specifically directs researchers toward atypical users, people who struggle
with existing services, existing evidence, and knowledgeable people inside and
outside the organization. This supports searching beyond active users, while
also warning that stakeholder opinion remains assumption until checked.

The OECD foresight toolkit adds a different mechanism: challenge assumptions,
consider multiple plausible futures, and seek courses robust or adaptable
across them rather than predicting one future. Reference-class forecasting
adds actual outcomes of similar undertakings where forecast claims permit a
defensible comparison. Neither lens completes the job: foresight generates
possibilities but does not rank them, and reference-class choice can dominate
the result.

The combined method therefore separates option discovery from work selection.
Reconstruct the whole beneficiary problem; deliberately vary beneficiary,
mechanism, channel, owner, and whether another organization should act; then
compare the current course and alternatives symmetrically. Preserve “none of
these,” contrary evidence, inaccessible groups, and disconfirming observations.
Small reversible probes are preferable where stories outrun evidence.

For `accessible-work-tools`, active users and feature requests can hide disabled
workers who abandoned the tool, support staff, and upstream workflow failures.
For `public-evidence-ledger`, the corpus can hide its own missing communities,
failed searches, and decisions made outside the ledger; federating or enabling
independent contributors may be alternatives to maintaining more claims. For
`reproducible-practice`, publication visibility can favor tractable topics while
deployment uncertainties and replication failures remain neglected.

These are reasoned transfers, not demonstrated effects. A later evaluation
would need the option set recorded before the pass, the discoveries made, and
subsequent beneficiary evidence, while guarding against hindsight and selection
of the most persuasive narrative.

2025, but the retained readings did not verify exact dates; their metadata
therefore records `unknown` rather than inventing precision.
