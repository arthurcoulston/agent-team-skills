# Worked transfer — choose the next accessible-work increment

Suppose the initial grant in the [accessible-work-tools
mission](../missions/accessible-work-tools.md) has funded two plausible next
increments: improve keyboard navigation in the existing tool, or recruit
people with less common low-vision workflows before building further.

Without deliberate selection, shipped features, tests and recent examples may
make the feature line dominate the record. Apply
[what is known about choosing work](choosing-work-under-a-directive.md): state
the beneficiary outcome each option could change, name the real alternative
declined, compare their bounded increments and opportunity costs, and set a
pre-result retirement or redirection condition. Research may be the better
choice when current evidence covers only already-recruited users, with the
choice reversing if recruitment cannot produce a decision-relevant contrast.

The transfer depends on comparable candidate work, a reassessment boundary and
observations from consenting beneficiaries. The evidence exposes repetition,
history-induced drift and weak mid-course selection; it does not say which
accessibility outcome matters, whether participants represent harder-to-serve
users, or whether either increment improves lived work. Those require mission
expertise and new evidence.

For [progress assessment](assessing-progress-under-a-continuing-mission.md),
record the prior choice and declined alternative, bounded action, external
observation, decision update and falsifiable next commitment. This trace makes
the transfer inspectable; it does not validate it.
