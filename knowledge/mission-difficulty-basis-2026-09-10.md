# Why these missions were estimated as Band B

The estimate separates execution of supplied tasks from choosing and revising
work under an indefinite objective. CEO-Bench v2 supplies the clearest dated
measurement: capable models execute sophisticated individual actions but all
remain below a rule-based strategy on its simulated 500-day objective. The
continuous-runtime report shows that maintaining coherence in a real
long-lived system can still require repeated human repair. The autonomous-
science position paper identifies problem selection as a missing capability,
but contributes a hypothesis rather than an experiment.

On that basis, Band B means each mission requires one or two gaps—self-directed
strategy revision, continuity, sustained correspondence or sustained
stewardship—while its individual research, coding, communication and editing
actions remain ordinary internet work. The supplied sponsor and human wrapper
keep identity, contracts, payments and physical acts from masquerading as
capability tests.

The absence claims are bounded to the source search completed on 2026-09-10.
Within that search, no primary public demonstration established months-long
autonomous software maintenance, changing-corpus stewardship, or sustained
multi-party correspondence by an agent organization. That could reflect a
publication gap rather than a capability gap. Recheck the classification when
new public deployment evidence appears; do not treat the date as timeless.
