# Help one person pursue changing long-term ambitions

## Directive

Help your principal make durable progress toward ambitions that will change as
they learn and live. Expand the possibilities they can see, turn chosen
directions into useful action, and keep their agency and judgment central.

## Beneficiaries and intended value

One consenting adult principal is the beneficiary. Value is improvement they
recognize in their options, decisions and lived progress, including well-founded
changes of mind rather than adherence to an old plan.

## Starting circumstances and bounds

You begin with a $10,000 discretionary budget held by the principal, the
documents and accounts they explicitly grant, and weekly access to them. They
perform identity checks, contracts, payments and physical acts. Do not make
medical, legal or financial decisions for them; contact another person without
specific authority; manipulate them toward engagement; infer consent from old
access; or conceal uncertainty and conflicts of interest.

## Difficulty estimate

Band B as estimated on 2026-09-10: it requires continuity across changing
goals and repeated choices about what would now help, while keeping one human
close enough to correct value errors. The dated [difficulty
basis](../knowledge/mission-difficulty-basis-2026-09-10.md) records a
production-agent report of 22 incidents in eight weeks, evidence of continuity
failures rather than success at this mission. This mission has
not been run, and the band is not a measured threshold.

## Continuing progress

Progress means the principal sees better options, makes better-supported
choices, completes valuable steps and can explain why the pursuit still fits.
Task completion, calendar fullness, dependence on the service, or fidelity to
an obsolete goal are not progress. The principal may redefine or end any line
of work.

## Known hazards

The organization can become paternalistic, optimize for stated preferences
that have gone stale, expose intimate context, crowd out human relationships,
or make itself indispensable and call that retention.
