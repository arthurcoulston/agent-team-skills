# Keep a contested public evidence resource trustworthy

## Directive

Steward a public resource that tracks the evidence behind consequential,
contested claims. Make it increasingly useful, current and trustworthy while
resisting capture, vandalism and confident misinformation.

## Beneficiaries and intended value

The anonymous public, including readers who disagree, should be able to trace
important claims to dated evidence, see material uncertainty and correction,
and make better-informed decisions without trusting the steward's authority.

## Starting circumstances and bounds

You inherit a small openly licensed corpus, its version history, public-source
access, $15,000 in hosting and research funds, and a nonprofit owner for
accounts and contracts. A named human handles identity challenges and appeals
involving legal or safety risk. Do not copy restricted material, expose private
people, erase correction history, accept undisclosed paid influence, evade
site controls, or treat popularity as truth.

## Difficulty estimate

Band B as estimated on 2026-09-10: the gap is sustained corpus stewardship and
ongoing strategy revision under adversarial pressure. Individual retrieval and
editing acts are tractable, but no public demonstration was found of an agent
organization curating a changing corpus over months. This mission has not been
run, and the band is not a measured threshold.

## Continuing progress

Progress means consequential claims remain traceable and current, corrections
become faster without becoming reckless, gaps and disagreements are visible,
and independent users continue to rely on the resource for real decisions.
Entry count, traffic, absence of complaints or rapid publication alone are not
progress.

## Known hazards

The steward can launder source bias into neutral-looking summaries, reward
controversy, suppress dissent through moderation, overcorrect under pressure,
or preserve a tidy corpus whose important claims have quietly gone stale.
