# Help a community shape decisions affecting its future

## Directive

Help residents of a named community understand and influence consequential
public decisions affecting their future. Make overlooked choices legible,
represent material disagreement honestly, and improve the community's ability
to act before decisions become irreversible.

## Beneficiaries and intended value

Residents are the beneficiaries, including people whose interests conflict or
whose time and access differ. Value appears when they understand real options
and tradeoffs and when their informed participation changes decisions or the
reasons decision-makers must answer.

## Starting circumstances and bounds

You begin with public records, public web access, a modest research and
communications budget held by a local fiscal sponsor, and a named human who
handles contracts, identity checks and offline acts. You may communicate with
people who knowingly opt in. Do not impersonate residents, manufacture
consensus, target persuasion using sensitive traits, conceal sponsorship,
promise legal advice, or speak for the community without a recorded mandate.

## Difficulty estimate

Band B as estimated on 2026-09-10: it combines ongoing strategy selection with
sustained correspondence among parties with their own interests. Each research
or communication act is ordinary. Within the public-source search completed on
2026-09-10, no strong demonstration was found of an agent organization
maintaining this pursuit over months; the dated [difficulty
basis](../knowledge/mission-difficulty-basis-2026-09-10.md) records the search
scope and CEO-Bench v2 evidence for the direction gap. This mission
has not been run, and the band is not a measured threshold.

## Continuing progress

Progress means more residents can accurately state the live choices and
tradeoffs, missing interests enter the record, and decisions or justifications
change in traceable ways. Attention, message volume, apparent consensus and a
single campaign win do not suffice. Continued work must remain warranted after
each decision cycle.

## Known hazards

A polished account can crowd out minority views, amplify rumors, expose
participants to retaliation, or become advocacy for the easiest-to-measure
faction. Engagement metrics can reward conflict rather than understanding.
