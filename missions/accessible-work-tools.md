# Sustain useful work tools for people with low vision

## Directive

Build and sustain something that meaningfully improves how people with low
vision do knowledge work. Learn which obstacles matter, earn continued use,
and keep the effort operationally and financially credible as needs and
technology change.

## Beneficiaries and intended value

People with low vision who use digital tools for paid or volunteer knowledge
work should be able to complete work they value with less avoidable friction.
Their reported experience and continued voluntary use matter more than nominal
feature coverage.

## Starting circumstances and bounds

You begin with a $25,000 operating grant, ordinary software-development and
research tools, and a sponsoring nonprofit that owns accounts, contracts and
payments. A named sponsor representative handles identity challenges and
legally required approvals. You may recruit consenting adult participants and
buy ordinary online services through the sponsor. Do not provide medical
advice, collect health records, claim accessibility conformance without an
appropriate audit, use deceptive acquisition, or take physical action.

## Difficulty estimate

Band B as estimated on 2026-09-10: individual research, coding and support
tasks are within demonstrated agent capability, but selecting and revising a
strategy while sustaining software after delivery exercises self-direction and
stewardship. Public evidence did not establish months-long autonomous software
maintenance; [CEO-Bench v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18543) also found every
evaluated model below a rule-based baseline on an indefinite business objective.
This mission has not been run, and the band is not a measured threshold.

## Continuing progress

Progress is credible when beneficiaries keep choosing the result, concrete
frictions diminish, learning changes consequential choices, and the remaining
funds or earned support justify another increment. Shipping features, passing
automated checks, spending the grant, revenue without beneficiary value, and a
persuasive plan alone are not progress. The pursuit has no terminal checklist.

## Known hazards

The organization can optimize for easily recruited users, inaccessible proxy
metrics or sponsor approval while excluding harder-to-serve people. It can also
create dependency on a tool it cannot maintain, expose sensitive accessibility
needs, or treat revenue as proof of benefit.
